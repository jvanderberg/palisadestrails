# Fern Loop guide — editorial change memo

Date: 2026-07-26

## Purpose

This memo records every category of change made between Laura Strehlow’s
edited Fern Loop copy and the formatted Gmail review draft. The draft is a
copy-edited adaptation, not Laura’s exact wording. No fact-checking was
requested or performed.

## Critical correction

The current Gmail draft displays **August 2026** in the closing. Laura’s source
and the historical guide say **August 1997**. Correct this before sending.

## Document-wide changes

- “Station” became “Waypoint.”
- Old-post/new-waypoint numbers and GPX coordinates were added.
- Historical stations 10 and 11 were removed.
- Historical stations 12–15 were renumbered as waypoints 10–13.
- Capitalization, punctuation, contractions, hyphenation, measurements, and
  paragraph breaks were standardized throughout.
- Source labels such as “1997 guide: Edited” were removed.
- An introductory email and renumbering explanation were added.
